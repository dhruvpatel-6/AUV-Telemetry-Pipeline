# 🛠️ Master System Integration Notes & Assembly Protocols
**Platform Iteration:** `v1.0.0-Prototype` | **Data Schema Standard:** `v3.0.0-Truth`

---

## 🔩 1. Mechanical & Actuator Integration
* **Mounting Clearances & Fasteners:** All 12 CubeMars AK80-9 actuators bolt directly into CNC 6061 aluminum leg linkages using M3 high-tensile fasteners. Medium-strength thread-locking fluid (Loctite 243) must be applied to every mounting screw to counteract high-frequency ground impact vibrations.
* **Pivot Joint Tolerances:** All leg joint pivot pins are machined to micrometer press-fit tolerances. Retaining lock-ring grooves are machined on each pin to prevent axial drift under lateral dynamic shear loads.
* **Hollow Linkage Conduit Clearance:** Internal sensor wires routed through hollow-core leg segments require a minimum of $3.0\,\text{mm}$ bend-radius clearance to prevent wire pinching or shearing during full mechanical leg extension and flexing.

---

## ⚡ 2. Power Distribution & Electrical Safety
* **High-Power Architecture:** Primary power is supplied via a 6S 22.2V 5000mAh 45C LiPo battery routed through a high-current copper distribution bus.
* **Inrush Anti-Spark Protection:** The primary power lead must pass through an XT90-S anti-spark connector before connecting to the distribution board to protect sensitive onboard compute logic from high-voltage inrush spikes during battery connection.
* **Multi-Rail Regulation:** A Matek Systems Duo BEC steps down the main 22.2V line into two regulated auxiliary rails:
  * **5V @ 5A Rail:** Dedicated exclusively to the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano single-board computer (SBC) and logic interface headers.
  * **12V @ 4A Rail:** Dedicated to active cooling fan assemblies.
* **Thermal Isolation:** The BEC buck converter is mounted directly to the aluminum frame chassis using thermally conductive pads, utilizing the structural frame as a passive heatsink.

---

## 📡 3. Bus Communications & Signal Integrity
* **CAN-Bus FD Topology:** Actuator communication runs on a 1 Mbps CAN-bus network daisy-chained across all 12 motor driver nodes.
* **Differential Line Shielding:** `CAN_H` and `CAN_L` signal lines are twisted at 1 twist per inch and encased in grounded copper mesh shielding sleeves to eliminate electromagnetic interference (EMI) generated by adjacent motor phase cables.
* **Impedance Matching:** Exactly two $120\,\Omega$ termination resistors are enabled—one at the Waveshare CAN HAT compute node and one at the final motor node on Leg 4—yielding a balanced total bus impedance of $60\,\Omega$ to prevent signal reflections.
* **Local Transducer Buses:** Joint thermistors and absolute encoders route locally back to motor driver nodes over isolated I2C and internal SPI lines, keeping low-voltage analog paths under $50\,\text{mm}$ in length.

---

## 🎯 4. Sensor Suite & Spatial Co-Location
* **CoM IMU Alignment:** The Bosch BNO055 9-axis IMU is mounted on a dampening plate co-located precisely at the calculated center of mass (CoM). This eliminates false angular acceleration offsets in state estimation algorithms.
* **Foot Tip Contact Transducers:** Interlink FSR 402 force-sensing resistors are integrated inside 3D-printed TPU rubber foot caps with $1.0\,\text{mm}$ of mechanical pre-compression clearance, ensuring instant digital ground-contact triggering without crimping signal wires.

---

## 🚨 5. Safety Interlocks & Emergency Stop (E-Stop)
* **Dual-Path Safety Architecture:** The spine-mounted emergency stop button routes through two simultaneous safety paths:
  1. **Software Interrupt:** Triggers an instant optocoupled GPIO signal directly to the Jetson SBC to freeze command trajectories.
  2. **Hardware Power Cut:** Drops the primary high-current MOSFET gate within $<15.0\,\text{ms}$, isolating motor power directly at the battery terminal level while keeping low-voltage compute logic energized.

---

## 💻 6. Software Architecture & Telemetry Contracts
* **Deterministic Control Loop:** Inverse Kinematics (IK) calculations execute on a real-time Linux environment (RT-Preempt) maintaining a strict $<5.0\,\text{ms}$ control cycle deadline.
* **HAL Abstraction:** The `HardwareAbstractionLayer.py` module encapsulates low-level hardware registers, exposing standardized APIs (`read_sensors()`, `dispatch_actuator_commands()`) to keep high-level control logic clean and decoupled.
* **Data Standard (`v3.0.0-Truth`):** All stream packets are schema-validated using `validate_contract()` before passing over local socket channels (`Ports 5555/5556`) to guarantee canonical JSON field typing.
* **Replay Logging:** System telemetry dumps continuously to an immutable `.jsonl` session log (`quadruped_session_recording.jsonl`) for post-run analysis and state replay.

## 🔌 7. Decoupled Ecosystem Attachment Contracts (Phase 7)
* **Zero-Modification Boundaries:** The core runtime loop does not execute third-party code directly. External systems plug in via clean JSON Schema contracts:
  * **TANTRA Observability:** Streams high-frequency telemetry via UDP on `Port 5557`.
  * **Replay Engine:** Receives sequence frames over TCP on `Port 5558`.
  * **Master Diagnostics Unit (MDU):** Injects diagnostic torque pulses over TCP on `Port 5559`.
  * **Automated Testing Suite:** Monitors latency metrics and fault states via UDP on `Port 5560`.
